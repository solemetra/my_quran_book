# Privacy Policy for Quran HD

**Last updated:** October 2026

**Quran HD** ("the App") is developed and maintained by Solemetra. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

### 1. Offline Operation & No Data Collection
The App functions 100% offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or behavioral analytics.

### 2. Local Storage
All your reading bookmarks, personal notes, Khatm reading goals, and settings are saved locally on your device only using Android local application storage. No data is ever uploaded to any cloud server or third-party service.

### 3. No Advertising & No Trackers
The App contains:
- No advertisements
- No analytics SDKs or telemetry frameworks
- No marketing or tracking modules
- No third-party data collection libraries

### 4. Permissions
The App requires zero sensitive permissions:
- No Internet permission
- No Camera permission
- No Microphone permission
- No Location permission
- No Contacts permission
